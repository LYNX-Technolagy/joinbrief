About

Built for the firm where everyone already knows each other's business.

Brief is for three to five people sharing a receptionist, a diary and a landline. Not a firm with a practice manager. Not a firm with an IT department. The kind of firm where the day's admin decides whether the day works, and a missed consultation is a lost client.

  1. 01

    One place, no spreadsheet.

    Consultations, depositions and court appearances live in the same view. Availability is read from what is already booked.

  2. 02

    Conflicts found before the booking.

    A double-booking is caught before it is confirmed. No damage control on the morning of the appearance.

  3. 03

    Alerts for what matters.

    A notification only arrives when something needs an answer — a clash, a confirmation, a task due. Nothing else reaches you.

Features

Three things, done properly.

No modules, no add-ons. What a firm of four actually uses, and nothing it has to switch off.

  • 01

    One place for the day's work.

    Consultations, appearances and tasks in a single shared view, per staff member and per day. Availability is read from that same view — nothing to reconcile, nothing to keep in a second list.

  • 02

    Conflicts caught early.

    If two events overlap for the same staff member or the same room, the booking is refused at the point of entry — not discovered the morning of the appearance.

  • 03

    Tasks, quietly.

    Light task lists attached to events, not floating in a separate system. Prepare the file, confirm the client, print the day view. Done, not discussed.
